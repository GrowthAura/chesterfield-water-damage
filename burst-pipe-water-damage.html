Which pipes freeze first in Chesterfield homes when a January cold snap hits?

Pipes in exterior walls, in bonus rooms over garages, in unheated basement corners and at outdoor hose bibs. Kitchen sinks on outside walls and bathrooms above garages are the most frequent failures.

Many two-story homes here have a room built over the garage with plumbing running through a floor cavity that sits above unheated space. When wind chills drop below zero, that cavity gets cold enough to freeze a line even with the furnace running.

First, stop the water

Find the main shut-off. It is normally on the basement wall facing the street, where the Missouri American Water line enters, next to the meter. Turn the handle clockwise, or a quarter turn if it is a lever.

Open a faucet on the lowest level to drain what is left in the lines.

Turn off the water heater if the main is off for long.

Switch off electricity to wet rooms if the panel is safe to reach.

Poke a small hole in a bulging ceiling with a screwdriver, with a bucket underneath, only if it is safe. It releases trapped water and can prevent a collapse.

The break you can see and the damage you cannot

A pipe that freezes usually bursts when it thaws, so the leak often starts hours after the cold has eased, sometimes while the house is empty. Water runs along joists and down wall cavities before it shows up. By the time a ceiling stain appears, insulation, the top of the wall below and the subfloor may all be wet.

We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to trace the full path, floor by floor, so drying equipment goes where the water is and not just where the stain is.

What repair involves

Extraction

Standing water is removed from floors and carpet. See Emergency Water Extraction.

Opening what must be opened

Saturated ceiling drywall and wet insulation are taken down. Wall bases are opened where needed so cavities can dry.

Drying

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until framing, subfloor and remaining drywall meet dry readings.

Putting it back

Drywall, texture, paint, trim and flooring are repaired. Ceilings are covered in more detail on our Ceiling Water Damage Repair page.

The pipe itself is repaired by a plumber. We coordinate so the wall is open once, not twice.

Vacant homes and travel

Some of the worst losses happen when owners are away for the winter or on a long trip. A frozen pipe water damage claim in Chesterfield, MO from an empty house can involve every floor because the water ran for days. If you are travelling in cold months, leave the heat at 55 degrees or higher, open sink cabinet doors on outside walls, and have someone check the house.

Types of pipe and how they fail

Copper splits along its length when ice expands inside it. PEX tolerates freezing better, but its fittings can fail. CPVC becomes brittle with age and cracks. Flexible braided supply lines to toilets and faucets fail at the crimp. Knowing which type failed helps the plumber and tells your insurer the cause.

Insurance

Sudden pipe breaks are among the most commonly covered water losses under a homeowners policy, though insurers may question a claim if the house was left unheated. We provide photos of the failed section, moisture maps and daily drying logs.
