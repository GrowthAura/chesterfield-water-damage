Can cupped hardwood floors in a Chesterfield home be saved without replacing them?

Often, yes. Cupping means the boards absorbed moisture from below. If they are dried slowly to their normal moisture level, many flatten out and only need sanding and refinishing.

The mistake is sanding too early. A cupped floor sanded flat while still wet will dip in the middle of each board, called crowning, once it finishes drying.

Reading the damage

Cupping: board edges higher than the center. Usually recoverable.

Crowning: center higher than the edges. Usually caused by sanding before drying was complete.

Buckling: boards lifting off the subfloor. Those boards are normally replaced.

Dark staining: water sat long enough to react with the wood. May sand out, may not.

Gaps after drying: boards shrank back. Often minor.

The floors in local homes

Site-finished solid oak

Common in entries, kitchens and great rooms. Nailed to a plywood subfloor. The best candidate for drying in place.

Engineered hardwood

A thin wood layer over plywood, often glued down. It can delaminate when soaked, and the wear layer limits how much it can be sanded.

Laminate and vinyl plank

Laminate swells and does not recover. Vinyl plank itself is waterproof, but water trapped under it has to be released by lifting sections.

Carpet over pad

In bedrooms and finished basements. Carpet is often saved after clean water. Pad is replaced.

How wood floors are dried

Extract surface water quickly.

Measure moisture in the boards and the subfloor, and compare with a dry area of the same floor.

Seal drying mats to the floor surface and connect them to a high-pressure blower that draws moisture up through the seams.

Run dehumidifiers in the room, and dry from below as well if the basement ceiling is open or can be opened.

Recheck daily. Wood is dried gradually over one to two weeks, because fast drying cracks it.

Sand and refinish once readings are stable.

Wet carpet

Wet carpet drying in Chesterfield, MO starts with extraction. With clean water, the carpet is lifted, the pad removed, the slab or subfloor dried, and new pad installed before the carpet is re-stretched and cleaned. Carpet that sat wet for more than two or three days, or was wet by contaminated water, is replaced.

The subfloor matters most

Plywood and OSB under the finished floor hold water long after the surface looks dry. OSB swells at the edges and may need replacing. If the floor is over a basement, we check from below too. Hidden moisture in the subfloor is what causes mold and squeaks months later. More on the drying phase is on our Structural Drying page.

Common causes

Dishwasher and refrigerator line leaks in the kitchen, described on Water Heater Leak Cleanup. Overflowing sinks and toilets. Burst pipes. Exterior doors and windows that let rain in. Pet water bowls and plant pots cause small local damage that does not need a crew.

Repair, refinish or replace

Water damaged floor repair in Chesterfield, MO falls into three levels. Dry in place and refinish. Replace the affected boards, weave in new ones and refinish the room so the color matches. Or replace the floor when most boards have buckled or the product cannot be sanded. We tell you which applies after drying, when the final condition is clear.

Cost and insurance

Drying a wood floor in one room typically runs from the high hundreds into the low thousands of dollars. Refinishing and board replacement add to that. Sudden leaks are usually covered by homeowners insurance, and insurers generally prefer drying to replacement when it is possible.

Floor questions

How long before cupping hardwood floors flatten?

Several weeks. Most of the moisture leaves during the drying period, and the boards continue to settle afterward.

Can I dry it with fans?

Fans dry the surface. They do not pull moisture from the underside of the boards or the subfloor.

Is wet subfloor drying possible without removing the floor?

Often, using mats from above and airflow from below.

Do you do carpet water extraction on upper floors?

Yes, with portable extractors.

Will the finish match?

When boards are replaced, the whole room is usually refinished so it does.
