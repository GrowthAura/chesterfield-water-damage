Which water damage service does a Chesterfield home actually need: extraction, drying or repair?

Most homes need all three in order. Water is extracted first, the structure is dried over several days, and damaged materials are repaired last. Skipping drying is what causes mold.

People often hear two terms. Mitigation means stopping the damage: removing water, taking out ruined materials and drying. Restoration means putting the building back: drywall, flooring, trim and paint. A water mitigation company that also rebuilds saves you from hiring twice.
