What should you do in the first 30 minutes when sewage comes up a Chesterfield basement floor drain?

Stop using all water in the house, keep everyone out of the area, shut the basement door, turn off the HVAC fan so air is not circulated, and call for professional cleanup.

Every flush, shower and washer cycle sends more water toward the blockage, and it comes back out at the lowest drain. Do not try to mop it up. Raw sewage carries bacteria and viruses that spread on shoes, hands and pets.

Why sewage backs up here

Your home connects to the public sewer through a pipe called the lateral. The public main belongs to MSD, the Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District. The lateral, from the house to the main, is the homeowner's responsibility.

Tree roots. Mature trees in established subdivisions send roots into lateral joints, where they catch paper and grease until the pipe closes.

Grease and wipes. Kitchen grease hardens in the line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down.

A sagging or collapsed lateral. Shifting clay soil can leave a low spot, called a belly, where solids settle.

Main line surcharge. During extreme rain the public main can run full and push back toward homes that have no backwater valve.

What makes this different from other water damage

Sewage is Category 3 water, the most contaminated class. That changes the rules for what can be saved.

Carpet and pad that touched sewage are removed.

Drywall and insulation that wicked sewage are cut out and removed.

Upholstered furniture, mattresses, rugs and cardboard are discarded.

Hard, non-porous items such as metal shelving, sealed wood and plastic can be cleaned and disinfected.

Concrete floors and framing are cleaned, disinfected and dried.

How we clean it up

Protection and containment

The crew wears protective suits, gloves and respirators. The affected area is sealed off with plastic sheeting so contamination is not tracked into the rest of the house.

Removal

Liquid and solids are extracted and pumped out. Contaminated materials are bagged and carried out along a protected path.

Cleaning and disinfection

All remaining surfaces are washed with detergent first, because disinfectant does not work through dirt, then treated with a disinfectant made for sewage losses.

Drying and air cleaning

Dehumidifiers and air movers dry the structure. HEPA air scrubbers run during the work to capture airborne particles and help with odor.

Rebuild

Once the area is clean and dry, walls and flooring are replaced.

Fixing the cause

Cleanup does not clear the pipe. A plumber needs to camera and clear the lateral, and repair it if it is broken. Ask about a backwater valve, which lets sewage flow out but not back in. If the blockage turns out to be in the public main, report it to MSD.

Odor

Sewage odor comes from material that is still there. Sprays and deodorizers do not fix it. When contaminated materials have been removed and the slab and framing have been properly cleaned and dried, the smell goes with them.

Sewage backup questions

Can I stay in the house during cleanup?

Usually yes, if the affected area is contained and you have a working bathroom on another drain line once the plumber clears the blockage. Households with infants, older adults or anyone with a weak immune system may prefer to stay elsewhere.

Is a floor drain backing up always sewage?

In most local homes the floor drain ties into the sanitary line, so treat it as sewage until shown otherwise.

Can raw sewage in basement carpet be steam cleaned?

No. Carpet and pad contaminated by sewage are removed.

How long does cleanup take?

Removal and disinfection are often finished in a day. Drying adds three to five days, and repairs follow.

What is category 3 water cleanup?

It is the cleaning standard used for the most contaminated water, including sewage and floodwater, and it requires removal of porous materials and disinfection.
