What should you do when the sump pump quits during a Chesterfield thunderstorm?

Check the breaker and the outlet first, then lift the float to see if the pump starts. If it does not run, move belongings off the floor and call for water removal straight away.

Do not stand in water to reach the pump, and do not plug it into an extension cord lying on a wet floor. If the power is out across the neighborhood, the pump cannot run unless it has a battery or water-powered backup.

Why sump pumps fail

Power outage

Summer storms in West County bring down lines, and an Ameren Missouri outage stops the pump at the moment the pit is filling fastest.

Stuck or failed float switch

The float is the part that tells the pump to turn on. It can jam against the pit wall, tangle in the cord or simply wear out.

Burned-out motor

Most pumps last roughly seven to ten years. One that has been running constantly through a wet spring can overheat and stop.

Frozen or blocked discharge line

In winter, the pipe that carries water outside can freeze where it exits the house. The pump runs, but water has nowhere to go and comes back into the pit.

Pump too small for the storm

A heavy downpour on saturated clay can send more water to the pit than a small pump can lift.

Dead backup battery

Backup systems only help if the battery has been replaced on schedule. Many have not.

How far the water goes

A sump pit is normally in the lowest corner of the basement, often inside a storage room or mechanical closet. When it overflows, water spreads under the wall plates into the finished side. From there it follows the slab to the lowest point, soaking carpet pad along the way. Homeowners often find a wet patch in the middle of the theater room and do not connect it to the pit behind the wall.

That is why we map moisture across the entire lower level instead of only around the pit.

Our cleanup steps

Get water moving out. If your pump is dead, we set our own pump in the pit to bring the level down and keep it down while we work.

Extract water from carpet, pad and hard floors.

Pull baseboards and check wall bases with a moisture meter.

Remove pad, and drywall or insulation where water wicked up.

Clean and apply antimicrobial treatment to the slab and wall plates.

Set air movers and dehumidifiers, then check them daily.

Repair the finished surfaces after drying.

The water from a sump pit is groundwater. It is generally treated as gray water, not sewage, but it carries soil and bacteria, so soaked pad is replaced and surfaces are cleaned. The wider process for lower levels is on our Flooded Basement Cleanup page.

Replacing the pump

A plumber installs the new pump. We can keep a temporary pump running in the pit so the basement does not flood again before the replacement goes in. When you talk to the plumber, ask about a battery backup, a high-water alarm and a check valve on the discharge line.

Will insurance pay for it?

This is the most misunderstood part of sump pump overflow cleanup in Chesterfield, MO. A standard homeowners policy usually does not cover water that backs up through a sump or drain. Coverage comes from an optional endorsement often called water backup and sump overflow, and it has its own limit. Check your declarations page for it. Either way, we document the cause, the affected rooms and the drying record. More on this is on our Water Damage Insurance Claim page.

Signs mold may follow

If the pump failed while you were away and the water sat for more than two days, expect some mold on the back of drywall and under pad. A musty smell that remains after the carpet feels dry is the usual warning. Our Mold Remediation page explains how that is handled.

Sump pump questions

My sump pump failed and the basement flooded. Is the carpet ruined?

Not necessarily. If the water was there less than a day or two, carpet can often be cleaned and dried. The pad is replaced.

The pump is running but the pit is still full. Why?

The discharge line is probably blocked or frozen, or the check valve has failed and water is falling back into the pit.

Is a sump pit overflow dangerous?

The water itself is a mild contamination risk. The bigger danger is electricity. Stay out if outlets or cords are wet.

My sump pump is not working but there is no water yet. Should I call?

Call a plumber for the pump now. If rain is forecast and water starts to rise, call us.
