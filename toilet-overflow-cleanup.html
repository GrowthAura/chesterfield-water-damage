Is an overflowing toilet on the second floor of a Chesterfield home an emergency?

Yes, if water left the bathroom floor, soaked carpet or reached the ceiling below. Water runs through the floor quickly, and overflow containing waste is contaminated and needs professional disinfection.

A small spill of clean water on tile that you wipe up within minutes is not. The dividing line is whether water got under the flooring or baseboards or through to the level below.

Stop the flow

Turn the oval valve behind the toilet clockwise until it stops.

If it will not turn, lift the tank lid and raise the float, or push the flapper down.

Do not flush again.

If other fixtures are also backing up, stop using water in the house. The main drain is blocked. See Sewage Backup Cleanup.

Clean water or contaminated?

From the tank or supply line

A cracked tank, a failed fill valve or a broken supply hose releases clean water. It is treated like any supply leak.

From the bowl, with no waste

A clog with clear water is gray water. Flooring and pad need cleaning and may need replacing.

From the bowl, with waste, or from the drain

This is Category 3. Carpet, pad and soaked drywall are removed and everything else is disinfected.

Where the water goes

In a second-floor bathroom, water runs to the edges of the room, under the baseboard and the vanity, and through the gap around the toilet flange and pipe openings. It collects on top of the ceiling below. Tile floors hide it, because the tile looks fine while the subfloor underneath is soaked.

We check the bathroom subfloor, adjoining closets and bedrooms, and the ceiling and walls of the room below with moisture meters and thermal imaging.

Cleanup steps

Extract water from all affected floors.

Remove contaminated carpet, pad and any saturated drywall or ceiling sections.

Pull the toilet if water got under the flooring around the flange.

Clean with detergent, then disinfect all affected surfaces.

Dry the subfloor, wall cavities and ceiling cavity with air movers and dehumidifiers.

Repair the ceiling, walls and flooring. See Ceiling Water Damage Repair.

Bathroom floors

Sheet vinyl and tile over plywood trap water. If readings stay high, a section of flooring is lifted to dry or replace the subfloor. Hardwood outside the bathroom door often cups a few days later. See Hardwood Floor Water Damage.

Why household cleaning is not enough

Mopping and bleach deal with the visible floor. They do not reach under baseboards, into the subfloor or the ceiling cavity, and they leave contamination and moisture where mold and odor develop.

If the ceiling below and flooring need replacing, it reaches the low thousands. Homeowners policies generally cover a sudden overflow from a fixture. A backup of the main sewer needs a backup endorsement.
