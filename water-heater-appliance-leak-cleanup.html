Why do water heaters in Chesterfield basements fail without warning?

Sediment builds up in the tank and the steel lining corrodes from inside. Most tanks last eight to twelve years, and the first visible sign is often water on the floor.

Because the tank is connected to the supply, it does not stop at 40 or 50 gallons. Water keeps flowing until someone closes the valve. In a finished basement the mechanical room usually shares a wall with carpeted space.

Shut it down

Close the cold-water valve on the pipe above the tank. If it will not close, shut the main.

For a gas unit, turn the gas control to off. For electric, switch off its breaker.

Do not touch standing water near an electric water heater until the breaker is off.

Leave the drain valve alone if the floor drain is not nearby.

Appliance leaks we clean up

Washing machines

Rubber supply hoses burst and drain hoses come loose. Second-floor laundry rooms are a particular risk because water goes through the floor into the ceiling below.

Dishwashers

Leaks from door seals, supply lines and drain connections run under cabinets and hardwood where they are not seen.

Refrigerators

The thin plastic or copper line to the ice maker cracks or pulls loose behind the unit and seeps into the subfloor for weeks.

Other sources

Water softeners, humidifiers on the furnace, reverse-osmosis units under the sink and AC condensate pumps.

The hidden part of an appliance leak

Kitchen leaks travel under cabinets and flooring. Toe-kicks hide water, and hardwood or vinyl plank traps it against the subfloor. We check with moisture meters across the room and into the basement ceiling below. Drying may involve removing toe-kicks, drilling small holes to direct air into cabinet bases, or using floor drying mats. Wood floors are covered on our Hardwood Floor Water Damage page.

Insurance and cost

Sudden appliance failures are generally covered by homeowners insurance. The appliance itself usually is not, and slow leaks that went on for months may be denied. Appliance leak water damage in Chesterfield, MO in a single room is typically in the hundreds to low thousands of dollars. Damage under hardwood or across two floors costs more.

Preventing a repeat

Replace rubber washer hoses with braided stainless ones. Put a drain pan and a leak alarm under the water heater and the washer. Note the age on the water heater label and plan to replace it around the ten-year mark. Check the ice maker line when you pull the refrigerator out to clean.

Leak questions

My water heater burst. Is the carpet next to it ruined?

Usually the carpet can be dried and the pad replaced, if it is handled within a day or two.

Is dishwasher leak water damage under hardwood fixable?

Often, with drying mats, if caught before the boards buckle.

How do I spot a refrigerator water line leak?

Soft or discolored flooring in front of the fridge, a stain on the basement ceiling below, or a musty smell.

Is water from a washer clean?

Supply-hose water is clean. Drain water is gray water and needs cleaning as well as drying.

How long does drying take?

Two to five days, depending on the flooring.
